01. Background

From First Lines of Python to Enterprise AI & Cloud

How a curious high schooler evolved into a certified ServiceNow & AI Developer building production-oriented architectures.

My journey in technology began in 12th grade (2023) when I wrote my first Python script. Driven by a passion for solving complex real-world problems, I enrolled in the B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science program at Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan College of Engineering (DSCE), Coimbatore.

What started as data processing and analytics quickly scaled into designing Agentic RAG Knowledge Assistants with Ollama & Llama 3.2. Recognizing the power of enterprise digital transformation, I expanded into the ServiceNow platform ecosystem, earning both the ServiceNow Certified System Administrator (CSA) and ServiceNow Certified Application Developer (CAD) global credentials.

Today, I bridge the gap between cutting-edge Generative AI models, automated enterprise workflows, and sustainable cloud computing infrastructure on AWS & Azure.
